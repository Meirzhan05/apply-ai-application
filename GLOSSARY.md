# Job Applications

This context describes the candidate information used to prepare job applications and match candidates with jobs.

## Language

**Profile**:
The candidate's editable personal and professional information used to fill job applications and support job matching.
_Avoid_: Resume, account

**Onboarding**:
The initial process in which a candidate provides a resume and supplies the information needed to establish their profile.
_Avoid_: Profile settings

**Current Location**:
The city, state or region, and country where the candidate currently lives.
_Avoid_: Preferred location

**Preferred Job Locations**:
The places where the candidate wants to work, which can differ from their current location.
_Avoid_: Current location

**US Immigration Status**:
The candidate's declared US citizenship, permanent-residency, visa-holder, or other status. This is separate from work authorization and sponsorship needs.
_Avoid_: Visa status, work authorization

**Visa Type**:
The visa category declared by a candidate who identifies as a visa holder.
_Avoid_: Work authorization

**Work Authorization**:
The candidate's explicit declaration of whether they are currently authorized to work in the United States.
_Avoid_: Visa status

**Sponsorship Need**:
The candidate's explicit declaration of whether they need employer sponsorship now or in the future.
_Avoid_: Visa status
